# Stock-Market-Analysis-Powerbi

Overview  
---
This project presents an interactive multi-stock market dashboard built to analyze stock performance, trends, and risk-return characteristics over time. The dashboard enables users to explore key financial   metrics, compare multiple stocks, and uncover actionable insights through dynamic visualizations.  

Objectives  
---
Analyze historical stock price movements
Compare performance across multiple stocks
Identify trends using moving averages
Evaluate market volatility
Understand risk vs return relationships  

Dataset
---
The dataset includes historical stock data with the following fields:  
Date  
Stock Symbol  
Open Price  
High Price  
Low Price  
Close Price  
Volume  

Time period covered: 2021 – 2026  

Key Features  
---
🔹 KPI Metrics  
Average Closing Price  
Maximum Closing Price  
Minimum Closing Price  
Total Volume

Visualizations  
---
 Candlestick Chart – Displays price movements (Open, High, Low, Close)  
 Moving Averages (20 & 50 days) – Identifies trends and momentum  
 Volume Analysis – Tracks trading activity over time  
 Market Volatility Chart – Measures fluctuations using rolling metrics  
 Risk vs Return Scatter Plot – Compares stock performance  
Year & Month Filters – Enables time-based analysis  
 Stock Selector – Compare multiple stocks dynamically   

 Insights Derived  
 ---
Stocks show cyclical trends with periods of high volatility  
Moving averages help identify bullish and bearish phases  
Certain stocks exhibit higher risk but also higher returns  
Volume spikes often align with significant price movements 

Tools & Technologies  
---
Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation  
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Calculated measures and KPIs  
Data Modeling – Relationships and filtering logic  

Key DAX Measures (Examples)  
---
Average Close Price  
Moving Average (20 days)  
Moving Average (50 days)  
Daily Returns  
Volatility (Rolling Standard Deviation)  

How to Use
---
Select a stock from the Stock Symbol filter  
Use Year / Month slicers to adjust timeframe  
Analyze:  
Price trends  
Volatility patterns  
Risk-return positioning
